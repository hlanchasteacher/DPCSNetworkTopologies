Case 03 Hybrid architecture · 8 workstations, Dept 1 [A–D] & Dept 2 [E–H]

Not checked
Paper diagram 3 — Section 3 of your sheet

Design and sketch a hybrid network connecting eight workstations split into two departments (A–D and E–H) that satisfies all four client constraints:

  • Constraint 1: Total build cost must not exceed $1,800.
  • Constraint 2: Zero data collisions are permitted anywhere on the network.
  • Constraint 3: Adding a future workstation to either department must cost no more than $100.
  • Constraint 4: If any single inter-departmental cable is severed (mark with ✂️), both departments must remain connected.
